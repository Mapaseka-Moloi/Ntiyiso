Why this is detectable

Narrowband 8 kHz G.711 strips the cues humans rely on — not the cues a model relies on. Generative vocoders leave a rigid glottal pulse train, no respiratory turbulence between phrases, and a spectral ceiling artefact above 3,2 kHz. Ntiyiso scores all four continuously, mid-call.

Why this wins — and where we're honest

— differentiation & limitations

What's different
1
Built for SA, on SA signals

Tuned for SA English, isiZulu, Sesotho, Afrikaans and 8 kHz low-bitrate trunks — the gap global players don't benchmark locally.

2
A warning signal, not an approval gate

We trigger step-up authentication instead of auto-blocking. Safer, and honest about detection limits.

3
Court-ready evidence

Explainability vectors, spectral/frame snapshots and a signed chain-of-custody hash per verdict.

Weakness → our strength
A
"AI keeps improving — hands look real now."

We never bet on one tell. We fuse many weak signals (temporal consistency, micro-expression timing, glottal-pulse statistics, GAN fingerprints) and, when unsure, escalate to a live human step-up.

B
"A brand-new caller with no history."

Our score is about the signal itself, not the account history — a synthetic voice or face is flagged even for a first-time caller, then a knowledge or liveness challenge closes the gap.

C
"Detection fails on unseen generators."

Correct — so we stream, re-score every second, and default to step-up when uncertain. Detection is continuous, not a one-shot pass/fail.
